# The iPhone app

> **[Get Timetap on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6828616436)** - "Timetap: Hours & Projects" (in
> Norwegian "Timetap: Timer og prosjekter"). One purchase, both versions included. iOS 17 or newer.

The app works two ways:

* **On the phone alone** - no server. Your hours, projects, weeks and months stay on the phone. Choose *Use on this
  phone* when you open it.
* **Connected to your Timetap** - the phone clocks you in and out, switches projects and reads tags, the same as a
  reader. Away from the office it keeps what you do and sends it when it reaches the server again; that part of the
  day is marked *remote*. When you get to work - you tap your tag on a reader, or the phone joins the work network -
  the remote part ends there and the rest of the day counts as at the office, without clocking out.

## Two versions in one app

**Settings > Version**: *Timetap Lite* - hours, projects and your time bank, for any country - or *Timetap* -
everything in Lite plus Norway's working-life rules: holiday days, self-certified and doctor's-note sick leave (also
part-time), days off for a sick child, paid overtime with your own overtime rule, rest between work days and the
working-time limits. Both are included; switch whenever you like, nothing is lost. A phone set to Norway starts on
Timetap. Connected to your Timetap server, the phone follows the server's version.

## Holiday, sick days and time off

On **Month**: **Select days**, tap the days, then **Mark as...** - holiday, sick, time off and the rest, or *Remove
marks*. A sick note with a doctor asks how much: 100 %, or part time (20-99 %). On a part-time sick day the day shows the
hours you worked, against what the sick note leaves for work. Month also shows the holiday days left this year and,
in Timetap, how much self-certified sick leave and sick-child days you have used.

Connected to a server, holiday that needs approval goes to an admin. If it's declined, the reason shows on Today and in
the holiday list on Month.

## Fixing a day

On **Week**, tap a work period to change its times or delete it, or use a day's **⋯** menu: *Add hours* (a forgotten
day), a **comment**, or **mark the day**. Connected, this changes your own hours on the server, the same as on the web
page. Away from the server, changed times, deleted periods, added hours and comments are kept on the phone and sent when
it reaches the server again; marking a day needs the server.

## Time bank and the monthly report

**Time bank** (on Today): how far over or under your hours you are. In Timetap, tap it for the days with extra hours -
mark them as paid overtime, priced by your own overtime rule - and the working-time limits. **Month > Monthly report
(PDF)** makes a report of the month to send your employer.

## Tags

**Settings > Tags > Pair a tag**, choose what the tag does, and hold the top of the phone to it. Timetap writes a small
link onto the tag, so afterwards holding an iPhone to it shows *Open in Timetap* - one tap, without opening the app first.
Use NTAG213, NTAG215 or NTAG216 tags (often sold as NFC stickers); other kinds can't hold the link. If the tag already
has something on it, or already does something else, the phone asks before replacing it.

* **On the phone alone:** *Clock in or out*, or a project.
* **Connected to your Timetap:** *My tag* - your own tag, which also clocks you in and out on the readers. Admins can
  also pair **project tags**: they work on everyone's phone and on the readers. A tag that was paired on a reader gets
  the link with *Add the link to a paired tag*. Someone else's person tag does nothing on your phone.

## Backups

* **Settings > Backup > Back up to iCloud Drive every day**: a copy in iCloud Drive > Timetap once a day when something
  has changed; the last 3 days are kept.
* **Save a backup** saves everything as one file whenever you like.
* **Restore from a backup** brings a backup back - also on a new iPhone, from the welcome screen. The iPhone's own
  iCloud Backup includes Timetap too.

## Connecting a phone

1. On the Timetap web page: **Account** > **Your iPhone** > **Connect a phone**. It shows an address and a code.
2. In the app: **Connect to Timetap**, type the address and the code within ten minutes, and **Connect**.

A lost phone: remove it under **Account** > **Your iPhone**.

## Widgets, Siri and automations

* **Widgets** on the Home Screen and Lock Screen show today's hours and clock you in and out.
* **Control Center and the Lock Screen** (iOS 18): add the *Clock in or out* control - or put it on the Action button.
* **Still clocked in?** Settings > *Remind me if I'm still clocked in*: if you're still clocked in a while after you
  usually finish (from *Your patterns*), the phone asks, with a **Clock out** button on the notification.
* **Siri and Shortcuts:** "Clock in with Timetap", "Clock in or out with Timetap", "Start a project in Timetap",
  "Today's hours in Timetap".
* **Clock in automatically:** with the Shortcuts app's automations the iPhone clocks you in and out by itself - when
  you arrive at work, when it joins the office WiFi, when you hold it to a tag (no tap at all), or at a set time.
  Timetap never sees where you are. The steps for each are under **Settings** > **Clock in automatically**.
